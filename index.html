Celebrating Young Creators

Kids Talent for Liberia brings children from all fifteen counties to showcase their gifts in music, dance, poetry and more.

Mission & Vision

Kids Talent for Liberia is more than a contest — it’s a movement. Our mission is to nurture the talents of children aged 4–19, promoting confidence, discipline and social cohesion. We envision a country where every child’s creative potential is celebrated and developed.

Competition Structure

The competition runs once a year and unfolds in two main stages: County Heats and the National Grand Final.

  • County Heats: Each county hosts auditions across disciplines—singing, dancing, poetry, storytelling, instrumental performance and visual art. Community panels select top performers to represent the county on the national stage.
  • National Grand Final: County champions gather in Monrovia to perform before a national audience. A panel of judges evaluates performances on skill, originality and cultural relevance.

Impact & Significance

Kids Talent for Liberia fosters cultural pride and brings communities together by celebrating diversity across Liberia’s fifteen counties. It builds confidence, educational opportunity and community engagement among young people.

  • Confidence & soft skills: Performing on stage builds self‑esteem and teamwork.
  • Educational opportunities: Winners receive scholarships and mentorship programs.
  • Community engagement: Competitions draw families and civic leaders together.
